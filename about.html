Built in Bihar for Bharat's businesses.

Connect Vyapaar is a B2B marketplace, digital growth agency and technology company in one, headquartered in Patna.

Who we are

One partner instead of ten vendors.

A small manufacturer or trader today deals with one company for listings, another for the website, another for ads, another for software, and a CA for compliance. None of them talk to each other, and nobody is accountable for growth.

Connect Vyapaar brings every link of that chain into one connected ecosystem. We list and verify your business where buyers search, bring you buyer requirements, run your marketing, and build the software that runs your operations, all with one team and one monthly report.

We start with the industries that drive trade in Bihar, such as construction materials, machinery, packaging, solar, furniture and agro-processing, and grow from there.

Our vision

To become India's most trusted growth infrastructure for small and medium businesses, starting from Bihar.

Our mission

One verified identity, one stream of qualified enquiries and one accountable growth team for every business we serve.

How we work

Principles we hold ourselves to.

These are the promises behind every plan, report and invoice.

Verified means checked

Our badge lists each check we did: GST, documents, site visit. Nothing vague.

Report, don't promise

No guaranteed rankings or leads. Every month we report what we did and what it delivered.

Your accounts, your data

Ad spend is billed to your own ad account. Your website, domain and data stay yours.

Buyers' privacy first

A buyer's number stays private until they choose a seller.

GST-ready billing

Every plan comes with a GST invoice so registered businesses can claim input credit.

Local team, national reach

A Patna team that visits your site, with a marketplace open to buyers across India.
