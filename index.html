Questions

Answers before you ask.

Is it free to post a requirement as a buyer?

Yes. Buyers post requirements free. We match your requirement with suitable sellers and you can compare up to three quotes side by side. Your phone number stays private until you choose to share it.

Can I list my business for free?

Yes. Every seller can create a free basic listing. Starter, Growth, Pro and Elite plans add more products, better search placement, a verified badge, marketing and an account manager.

What does the Connect Verified badge mean?

The badge shows exactly what we checked: your GST registration, business documents and a visit to your premises. Buyers can see which checks were completed.

Do you guarantee rankings or a fixed number of leads?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee search rankings or lead numbers. We commit to the work in your plan and send a report every month showing what we did and what it delivered.

Who pays for advertising?

Ad spend on Google, Meta and LinkedIn is billed directly to your own ad account, so you always see exactly where the money went. Our fee covers planning and management.

Are prices inclusive of GST?

Plan prices are shown before GST. 18% GST is added on the invoice, and registered businesses can claim input tax credit.

How long does a website take?

A standard mobile-ready business website is delivered in 21 working days after we receive your content and approvals.

Do you only work with businesses in Bihar?

We started in Patna and focus on Bihar first, but we work with businesses across India, and the marketplace is open to buyers from anywhere.
