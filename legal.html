Privacy policy

Connect Vyapaar Private Limited ("we") collects the information you give us — name, business name, phone, email, city and requirement details — to match buyers with sellers, respond to enquiries and provide our services. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

How we use your information

  • To share a buyer's requirement with matched sellers. A buyer's phone number is shared only after the buyer chooses a seller.
  • To send enquiry alerts, reports and service updates by WhatsApp, SMS or email.
  • To verify sellers (GST, documents, site visit).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by writing to connectvyapaarofficial@gmail.com.
